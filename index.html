Field notes

1881 Page Mill Road
Industrious Palo Alto Page Mill is at 1881 Page Mill Road, Suite 100: a standalone building at the gateway to Stanford Research Park, with a shared outdoor terrace and on-site parking. Industrious · Industrious blog
Page Mill Hill
Listed as a Class A campus of four two-storey buildings, LEED Gold, with 577 parking spaces. Façade numbers in listing photos show 1881 and 1891 as two joined wings, which OpenStreetMap draws as one footprint; the 1881 address point falls inside it. CommercialCafe listing · OSM address point
Stanford Research Park
Built from 1951 as Stanford Industrial Park; landscape architect Thomas Church designed its street medians. PCAD
What is illustrative
Measured from OpenStreetMap: footprints, streets and the junction, the pond, its fountains and benches, mapped tree positions, traffic signals. Read from listing photos: silver metal-panel wings with ribbon glass, white walls and red tile roofs on the older wings, redwoods and pines by the pond. Drawn by eye: most heights (only 1801 has mapped storeys), the species at each tree position, the woodland fill, house colours across Page Mill Road, people, traffic and the evening lights.
